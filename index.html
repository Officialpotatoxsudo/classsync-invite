About the app

One quiet Android space for your class routine, shared photos, and lab reports.

Built for a friend group in Nepal so nobody digs through messy chat threads for whiteboard photos, schedules, or lab write-ups.

Weekly routine & live widget

Your section timetable ships ready to use with live class progress, teacher contacts, and a home-screen countdown widget.

Auto-tagged shared gallery

Snap whiteboard photos or quick notes during class and they file automatically under the active subject, cached offline.

Lab report builder & PDF export

Draft report sections with AI from your notes and captions, attach class photos, and export clean PDFs on-device.

Assignments & solo mode

Track deadlines by subject and start right away with “Continue without a class” before classmates even join.
